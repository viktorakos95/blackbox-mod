blackbox-mod patcher

Unofficial mod for the original 1010music Blackbox, firmware 3.1.9. Pick what you want, choose your own stock BLACKBOX.bin, and the patched file is made in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Experimental, little tested on real units. The patched file is about 1.7 MB (stock is 0.7 MB) and some installers may refuse it ("unable to verify" or "firmware not found"): copy it to the card again, and if it still fails, install your stock file.

What's new
    Good to know (opening pages, saving, CPU, memory)

    Finding the pages

    • Press SONG to step through the pages you picked, in this order: Looper, SAMPLR, Trigger2MIDI, DRONE-8, MCL, then the normal song screen. BACK steps back. A page you didn't pick is skipped.
    • The Looper also opens with MIX, after Mixer, Mute and Solo.

    Saving

    • SAVE (on the Looper's SETUP page, on SAMPLR and on DRONE-8) writes the Looper's options, DRONE-8's settings and Trigger2MIDI's settings to the card with your project.
    • Not saved: the loops you record, and SAMPLR's own settings.

    Processing power (CPU)

    • The Blackbox has a fixed amount of audio processing power, and each machine takes a share on top of what the Blackbox already uses. Many ringing pad voices with Fidelity and Saturation, DRONE-8, and Looper tracks with effects all add up.
    • Grain sounds are among the hungriest: the Looper's GRAIN and SMOOTH pitch settings, and SAMPLR's Grain mode (the more fingers, the more grains).
    • When the load gets high, nothing is cut off suddenly: parts fade out slowly. Looper tracks fade out and are muted (the page shows it), SAMPLR tracks drop out, and DRONE-8 gives up its delay lines first. They come back by themselves when there is room again, or straight away when you open that page.
    • The machine whose page you opened last is the last to give way.
    • DRONE-8's MIX page shows the audio load at the top while you aren't turning anything. Opening big folders or samples on the SD card lowers the limits for a moment.
    • If the sound starts to drop out: use fewer Looper tracks with effects, less Grain, or fewer ringing pad voices.

    Memory

    • Looper tracks hold about 15 seconds each (four tracks). The Looper takes about a third of the memory the Blackbox uses for samples and recordings, so very big projects may have less room.

    Looking after your Blackbox

    • Keep your stock BLACKBOX.bin and back up your SD card before updating. Use at your own risk.
